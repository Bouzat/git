==About==
The '''Brawl Stars World Finals 2025''' is the concluding event of the [[Brawl Stars Championship/2025|Brawl Stars Championship 2025]] and the seventh annual edition of the ''Brawl Stars World Finals''. The qualifying format utilizes a point system based on official regional ''Monthly Finals'' placements to determine the qualified teams, similar to preceding events.

===Format===
* '''Participants'''
** Twelve best teams from different regions qualify directly by BSC points. Slots are allocated as follows:
*** [https://liquipedia.net/brawlstars/Brawl_Stars_Championship/2025/Leaderboards/North_America North America] - three slots
*** [https://liquipedia.net/brawlstars/Brawl_Stars_Championship/2025/Leaderboards/South_America South America] and [https://liquipedia.net/brawlstars/Brawl_Stars_Championship/2025/Leaderboards/East_Asia East Asia] - two slots each
*** [https://liquipedia.net/brawlstars/Brawl_Stars_Championship/2025/Leaderboards/EMEA EMEA] - five slots (including one won during the [https://liquipedia.net/brawlstars/Brawl_Stars_Championship/2025/Brawl_Cup Brawl Cup])
** {{bgcolortext|up|Four teams}} qualify through the [https://liquipedia.net/brawlstars/Brawl_Stars_Championship/2025/Last_Chance_Qualifier#About Last Chance Qualifier]<br><br>
* '''Group Stage''' - ''November 28, 2025 - November 29, 2025''
** Four Double-Elimination Format (GSL) Groups
** Each group has 4 teams
** All matches are {{Abbr/Bo5xBo3}} 
** {{bgcolortext|up|Two teams}} from each group advance to Playoffs<br><br>
* '''Playoffs''' - ''November 30, 2025''
** Single-elimination bracket
** All matches are {{Abbr/Bo5xBo3}}
